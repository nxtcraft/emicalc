What is EMI?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Instalment. It is the fixed amount you pay to the bank every month to repay a loan. Each EMI has two parts — a principal component (the amount that reduces your loan) and an interest component (the bank's charge for lending).

In the early months of a loan, most of your EMI goes toward interest. As you keep paying, the principal component gradually increases. This is called an amortizing loan.

EMI Formula

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)ⁿ / ((1 + r)ⁿ − 1)

  • P = Principal loan amount
  • r = Monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100)
  • n = Loan tenure in months

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the current home loan interest rate in India?

Home loan rates in India typically range from 8.5% to 10.5% per annum depending on the bank and your credit score. SBI, HDFC, ICICI, and Axis Bank are major home loan lenders. Use our home loan EMI calculator to calculate your EMI at any rate.

How can I reduce my loan EMI?

You can reduce your EMI by: (1) increasing the loan tenure — longer tenure means smaller EMI but more total interest; (2) negotiating a lower interest rate with your bank; (3) making a larger down payment to reduce the principal; (4) making part-prepayments during the loan to reduce the outstanding balance.
