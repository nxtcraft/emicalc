Car Loan EMI Calculator — Everything You Need to Know

A car loan EMI depends on three things: the loan amount (usually 80–90% of the car's on-road price), the interest rate offered by the bank, and the repayment tenure (typically 1–7 years for cars).

Frequently Asked Questions

What percentage of car price can I get as a loan?

Most banks finance up to 80–90% of the on-road price. Some banks offer 100% financing for salaried customers with a good credit score. The rest must be paid as a down payment.

What is the maximum car loan tenure?

Most banks offer car loan tenure from 1 to 7 years. Some NBFCs offer up to 8 years. Longer tenure means lower EMI but more total interest paid.

Can I foreclose a car loan early?

Yes. Most banks allow foreclosure after 6–12 EMIs. There may be a prepayment penalty of 1–5% of the outstanding amount on fixed-rate car loans.
