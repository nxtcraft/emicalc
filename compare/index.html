How to Compare Loan Offers

When comparing two loan offers, do not just look at the interest rate. Consider the EMI, total interest payable, and the tenure. A loan with a slightly higher rate but shorter tenure may cost less in total interest than a lower-rate loan with a longer tenure.

  • Same loan amount? The one with lower total payment wins.
  • Different amounts? Compare the total cost relative to what you borrow.
  • Always factor in processing fees (0.5–2% of loan amount) which are not shown here.
