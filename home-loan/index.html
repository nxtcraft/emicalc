Tips to reduce your home loan EMI

  • Make a larger down payment (reduces principal)
  • Negotiate a lower interest rate (check multiple banks)
  • Extend the tenure (lower EMI but more total interest)
  • Make part-prepayments when you have extra funds
  • Transfer your loan to a lower-rate bank (balance transfer)

Frequently Asked Questions

How is home loan EMI calculated?

EMI = P × r × (1+r)ⁿ / ((1+r)ⁿ−1), where P = principal, r = monthly rate (annual rate ÷ 1200), n = tenure in months.

What is the EMI for a ₹30 lakh home loan at 9% for 20 years?

The monthly EMI for a ₹30 lakh home loan at 9% for 20 years is approximately ₹26,992. Total interest paid over 20 years is about ₹34.78 lakh.

What is the EMI for a ₹50 lakh home loan?

At 9% for 20 years: ₹44,986/month. At 8.5% for 20 years: ₹43,391/month. At 9% for 30 years: ₹40,231/month.

Can I pay off my home loan early?

Yes. You can make part-prepayments at any time. RBI rules say banks cannot charge prepayment penalty on floating rate home loans. Fixed rate loans may have a small prepayment fee.
