💡 Reduce Tenure vs Reduce EMI: Reducing tenure (same EMI, shorter loan) almost always saves more money than reducing EMI (same tenure, lower monthly payment). Choose "Reduce EMI" only if your monthly cash flow is tight.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is EMI?

EMI (Equated Monthly Instalment) is the fixed monthly payment for a loan. It includes principal repayment and interest. EMI = [P × R × (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N – 1], where P = principal, R = monthly interest rate, N = tenure in months.

When is the best time to make a prepayment?

As early as possible. In the first few years of a loan, almost all of your EMI goes toward interest. A prepayment at year 3 saves much more than the same prepayment at year 15. Banks typically allow one free prepayment per year on floating rate loans.

Does reducing tenure vs reducing EMI matter for tax benefits?

Your Section 80C deduction (up to ₹1.5L on principal) and Section 24B deduction (up to ₹2L on interest) both remain the same regardless of whether you reduce tenure or EMI. The tax benefit calculation depends on what you actually pay that year — the Tax Benefit tab in EMICalc shows the breakdown.
